What this project is and needs to become. 

We have a large company, this company consists of 8 transport companies, 8 property companies, two investment companies and one administrative company that manages the fininances and admin of all other companies.  CompassKB is a tool that is used by the admin company.  As there are 16+ companies part of this group there is a considerable amount of administration that needs to happen on a daily bases.  
CompassKB should be a tool that any admin person can use to assist them in their daily tasks.  it should be a browser bassed web application using the Laravel framework, Tailwind CSS and MySql.  It should look similair to what most LLM chat windows look like.  Lets take an example of how an admin person will use this application. 

Jane (admin staff) receives a supplier statement from one of the transaporters suppliers.  She needs to raise supplier invoices in the form of purchase orders on our accounting software but Jane cannot remember what is the correct procedure to follow when raising purchase orders for this supplier.  Jane then opens CompassKB and in the chat prompt she writes:  "I need to create purchase orders for Supplier XYZ.  What are the GL accounts i need to use, and what are the tax defaults i needto use to create these purchase orders?" CompassKB then replies with the needed information and links that Jane can click on that will direct her to the physical SOP's for these processes.  
Jane then uses this information to process the purchase orders.  Another example,  Sally is head of Health and Safety, an employee has just been injured on duty.  Saly prompts CompassKB, "I need all of the required documents to complete an IOD that has just occurred".  CompassKB then provides her with the neccessary documents and instruct her on the correct SOP.  Lets take one other example.  A new 
vehicle has just been purchased for one of the transport companies.  An admin staff member needs to add this vehicles information to the accounting software.  Johan promps CompassKB, "Show me the information for ADV001", CompassKB then supplies Johan with all of the documents relating to this vehicle.  Another example would be Peter in die debtors department (also admin staff) needs to raise a Sales Order for a trip that one of the transporters have completed for a client.  He raises the sales order but now he suspects that he has made an error on the invoice. (All invoices are saved locally where CompassKB has access to them in pdf format).  Peter prompts CompassKB, "I want you to show me all sales invoices for client IXM that was done in August 2026 and then indicate which of those invoices does not have a unit cost of 18.16".  Compasss KB then lists all the invoices (Clickable links that when Peter clicks on one it displays the invoice) and then highlights those that have the incorrect information.
